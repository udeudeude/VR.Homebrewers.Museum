Historical Model Viewer

Load a recovered Wavefront OBJ model to examine its original polygon geometry. This is a stereoscopic viewer, not the original program or its materials, animation, or lighting.

Choose an OBJ model. Touch-drag rotates it; head tracking is available on supported phones.

Back to museum. For iPhone head tracking, open the hosted HTTPS page in Safari and grant motion permission. This viewer does not yet apply Cardboard lens-distortion calibration.

Historical demonstrations

BRender: Original Argonaut rendering examples. DOS programs require compatible assets and configuration.

Virtus Player: Historical world browser; original environments and Windows compatibility need investigation.

Magic Carpet: Commercial game demo. Original gameplay must be emulated; a separate stereo renderer would require deeper engineering.

CyberMaxx: Headset tracking interface source code, not itself a virtual world.

Preservation approach

Original program execution first; original assets and source-based ports where emulation cannot support Cardboard. No fabricated claim of running historical demonstrations in stereo.
